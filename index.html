A proposal for nanoaci.com

NanoACi Companion

One AI layer across the patient journey. Screening in, evidence out, clients back in. Not a campaign, a system, presented with a working prototype.

01 · The read on the brief

The scarce resource isn't attention. It's complete follow-up data from 100 consecutive patients who fly home.

NanoACi's own site says "suitability before salesmanship" and stakes its future on the NanoACi 100 evidence plan, from technique paper to multicentre work. A promotion push before the outcomes exist would undercut that positioning. So the same principle, applied to this proposal:

Their standard

Keep the surgeon. Keep the science. Remove the arthroscopy.

This offer

Keep the positioning. Skip the campaign. Build the proof, and let it recruit.

02 · The 360 loop

Evidence in, clients out. One patient data layer underneath.

The front door is a tool. The candidacy check turns site visitors into qualified, imaging-ready enquiries, in their own language, in the clinic's own voice.

Their own AI can join the loop. Check-ins reserve a slot for MAI Motion movement tests, pairing objective biomechanics with patient-reported scores.

Every patient recruits their physio. The shared recovery record turns each local clinician into a first-hand witness, then a referral channel.

04 · Plumbing, not prompts

The stack connects platforms they already own.

MAI Motion
their platform
A reserved registry slot for objective movement scores at each timepoint. Whether MAI Motion fills it is the practice's call, through their interface.
WhatsApp Business API
delivery
Check-ins arrive where international patients actually reply. SMS and email fallback, completion tracked per channel.
REDCap
registry of record
Research-grade capture with audit trails and exports journals recognise. Publication-ready by construction.
Secure DICOM exchange
imaging intake
Scans reach remote review with the candidacy summary, before any travel decision, without anyone emailing files.
LLM API layer
conversation only
Phrasing, translation, free-text parsing. Clinical questions, scoring and escalation stay frozen in plain, testable code outside the model.
React widget + FastAPI + Postgres
application
Embeddable candidacy check on nanoaci.com, internal dashboard, scheduler pinned to each patient's timepoints. AWS London, DPIA first.

Running cost at 100-patient scale: low hundreds of pounds per month.

05 · The offer, in numbers

We commit to what the platform controls. Measured, not assumed.

90%+
Follow-up completion target at every timepoint, against the 60 to 70% typical for questionnaires. Stage-gated on the first 15 patients.
<10%
Attrition across the full 100-case series, including complications and non-responders.
7 days
From first contact to a remote-review decision, so nobody books a flight on hope.
2×
Qualified, imaging-ready enquiries from the same traffic. A target measured against a month of baseline instrumentation, not a promise.
<60 min
An instant safety-net message fires on any red flag; a human follows within the hour in working time. The AI never manages a complication.
30 min+
Staff time saved per patient per timepoint on follow-up admin.

What we refuse to promise: clinical outcomes. Discovering those is the registry's whole job, and promising them would break the exact credibility this brand is built on.

06 · How it ships

Highest evidence value first.

Recovery companion
2 weeks
Build-complete and sign-off-ready: working software, full question set, escalation pack, draft DPIA. First patient then moves at governance speed.
Registry dashboard
4 weeks
Cohort statistics, review queue, multilingual check-ins. REDCap sync replaces manual export.
Candidacy check
4 weeks
Embedded on nanoaci.com, feeding the same data model, with secure imaging hand-off to remote review.
The growth loop
ongoing
Public outcomes counter, milestone content, MAI Motion integration, professionals referral view.

Honest constraints, designed in from day one: outcome data is special-category data under UK GDPR, every patient-facing word needs clinical sign-off, clinical-safety casework (DCB0129/0160) and a formal medical-device qualification check happen before the first patient, and the software presents data only - what to do about it is never written by code. That discipline is why this works for a "keep the science" brand.
